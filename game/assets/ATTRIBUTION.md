# Foundation placeholder asset provenance

The nine SVG block textures under `blocks/` were created specifically for
Craft-and-Defend during F2 on 2026-09-11. They are original project content and
do not copy Minecraft, MinionClash, Voxel Tools sample, or third-party artwork.

They are deliberately simple identification textures for the Foundation gate,
not final art or a representation of production visual quality. Tony retains
the project-content licensing decision.
